# CONTROL CENTRE — Master Plan
### Business Operating System for A P Technologies (GFRP / BFRP Rebars)

> One place from which the owner can see and control every part of the business: Marketing, Sales, Operations, Finance and Team.

---

## 1. Understanding the Business

### 1.1 What A P Technologies does
- **Manufactures and trades** GFRP (glass fibre) and BFRP (basalt fibre) rebars — ribbed and sand-coated.
- **Markets:** NHAI / MoRTH highways, bridges & culverts, drainage, sewage (STPs, manholes, pipes), coastal and marine structures, and other civil works where steel corrosion is a problem.
- **Value proposition:** does not rust, about 1/4 the weight of steel, high tensile strength, lower life-cycle cost, no cover-concrete spalling, and non-conductive / non-magnetic.

### 1.2 How the business actually runs (the value chain)

```
 RAW MATERIAL          PRODUCTION                QUALITY             SALES & DELIVERY         MONEY
 ─────────────         ──────────────            ─────────           ────────────────         ─────
 Glass / Basalt   →    Pultrusion line      →    Tensile, bond, →    Quote → PO → Dispatch →  Invoice → Collection
 roving                (fibre + resin pull)      diameter, glass     E-way bill, transport    (often 60–180 day credit,
 Resin (epoxy /        Rib winding / sand         content, Tg,       Site delivery, bends     retention, TDS, GST)
 vinyl ester)          coating → curing           water absorption   & stirrups pre-made
 Hardener, sand,       Cutting to length          Batch test cert    (GFRP can't be bent
 fillers, packing      Bending (factory only)     NABL 3rd-party     at site)
```

### 1.3 What makes this business different from a normal manufacturer
Any system we build has to handle these:

| Characteristic | What it means for Control Centre |
|---|---|
| **Specification sale.** A buyer cannot just buy it. A consultant, Authority Engineer or design engineer must **approve** GFRP for the project first. | The CRM has to track *influencers* (consultants, PMCs, designers, NHAI officers) separately from *buyers* (EPC contractors). It also has to track the approval stage of each project. |
| **Long sales cycle** (3–18 months) for infra projects | Pipeline by **project**, not just by lead. Each project has stages, decision makers and expected tonnage or metres. |
| **Government / tender-driven** (NHAI, municipal corporations, Jal Board, smart city, GeM) | A tender tracker with deadlines, EMD, documents and bid results. |
| **Proof-driven buying.** Test certificates, NABL lab reports, case studies and code compliance (ACI 440, ASTM D7957, and the applicable BIS/IRC codes) | A document library: every batch linked to its test report, and every quote able to attach the right certificates. |
| **Steel is the competitor**, not just other GFRP makers | A Steel → GFRP **substitution and savings calculator** (diameter equivalence, weight, cost, life-cycle). This is the main marketing and sales tool. |
| **Made-to-order lengths, bends and stirrups** | Orders need a cut/bend schedule (BBS). Production planning follows order line items. |
| **Raw material price swings** (resin, glass fibre) | Live costing per metre for each diameter. The minimum selling price updates when RM prices change. |
| **Long credit and retention** from EPC contractors | Strong receivables: ageing, promised dates, owner per invoice, and escalation. |
| **Coastal / geographic focus** | Map of projects and leads by region and coastline belt to plan field coverage. |
| **Accountability gaps in the team** | Every lead, order, task and invoice has **one owner, one due date and one status**. Overdue items escalate automatically and everyone has a visible scorecard. |

## 2. Design Principles of Control Centre

1. **One owner per thing.** No lead, task, order, batch or invoice is left without an owner and a due date.
2. **If it's not in Control Centre, it didn't happen.** WhatsApp, calls and Excel become inputs, never the record.
3. **Mobile first.** Field sales, the shop floor and dispatch all work from phones. Forms must take under 60 seconds.
4. **Numbers, not opinions.** Every role has 3–5 KPIs that the system calculates on its own.
5. **Daily rhythm built in.** A morning plan, an evening report and a weekly review are all generated by the system.
6. **Don't replace what works.** Keep Tally (or the current accounting software) for statutory books. Control Centre sits on top and pulls or pushes data.
7. **Build in phases.** Each phase goes live, gets used and then gets improved before the next one starts.

## 4. Modules in Detail

### 4.1 TEAM & ACCOUNTABILITY (the backbone, built first)
- **Org chart and roles:** who reports to whom, and what each role owns.
- **KRA/KPI sheet per person**, for example:
  - Sales Executive: new leads/week, site visits, quotes sent, conversion %, collections.
  - Production Supervisor: metres produced per shift, scrap %, downtime hours, plan adherence %.
  - QC: batches tested on time, NCRs closed, certificate turnaround.
  - Accounts: invoices raised within 24 hrs of dispatch, collection vs target, overdue >90 days.
- **Task engine:** tasks are assigned with owner, due date and priority, then move through accepted → in progress → done → verified. Overdue tasks escalate (Day 0 owner → Day 2 manager → Day 4 owner of the business).
- **Daily Work Report (DWR):** a 1-minute mobile form (plan in the morning, actuals in the evening), auto-filled from the system where possible.
- **Meeting system:** weekly review agenda generated from the data. Minutes of meeting (MoM) are recorded and every action point becomes a tracked task.
- **Scorecard:** a weekly red/amber/green card for each person, used for reviews and incentives.

### 4.2 SALES
- **Lead → Enquiry → Project → Quotation → Order** pipeline.
- **Project-centric CRM:** each infra project records its authority (NHAI, PWD, municipal corporation), EPC contractor, consultant/AE, location, quantity (metres/MT), stage (Identified → Spec discussion → Sample/Trial → Approved → Quoted → Ordered → Supplied) and an expected date.
- **Influencer tracking:** consultants and design engineers, with the projects they influence and the approvals they gave.
- **Quotation builder:** diameter-wise price list, automatic calculation of GST, freight and bends/stirrups, approval rules for discounts below a floor price, and a PDF with test certificates attached.
- **Tender tracker:** source (GeM / CPPP / state portals), due date, EMD/BG, documents checklist, bid value, L1/L2 result, and reason lost.
- **Follow-up discipline:** every open lead must have a next follow-up date. If it's missing or overdue, it shows up on the salesperson's and manager's dashboard.
- **Dealer / channel management** (trading side): dealer list, stock with dealer, dealer orders and targets.

### 4.3 MARKETING
- **Steel ↔ GFRP calculator** (public on the website + inside the system): equivalent diameter, weight, cost, transport savings and a 50-year life-cycle cost comparison. It captures the visitor's details as a lead.
- **Lead source tracking:** website, IndiaMART/TradeIndia, exhibitions, referrals, consultant meets, tenders, LinkedIn. Cost per lead and conversion per source.
- **Specifier programme:** a target list of consultants, PMCs, NHAI regional offices and municipal engineers, with a visit plan for technical presentations and CPD seminars.
- **Content & proof library:** case studies, project photos, test reports, compliance documents, brochures and installation guides, so the team always shares the latest version.
- **Campaign tracker:** exhibitions, seminars, digital campaigns, the budget for each, and the leads and revenue generated.
- **Coastal / regional map:** leads, projects and competitors plotted on a map to plan coverage.

### 4.4 OPERATIONS
- **Order → Production plan:** confirmed orders become a production schedule by line, diameter and shift, with a cut/bend schedule (BBS) for each order.
- **Production log (per shift, per line):** metres produced, diameter, resin and roving consumed, scrap, downtime with reasons, and operator.
- **Batch / lot traceability:** each batch is linked to its RM lots, line, date, QC result, the customer order it went to, and its dispatch.
- **Inventory:** raw material (roving, resin, hardener, sand, packing), WIP, finished goods by diameter and length, reorder levels and alerts.
- **Purchase:** indent → PO → GRN, a vendor list with rates history, and lead times.
- **Dispatch:** packing list, vehicle, LR, e-way bill reference, delivery confirmation (photo/POD) and the link to the invoice.
- **Machine maintenance:** preventive maintenance schedule for pultrusion lines and dies, and a breakdown log.

### 4.5 QUALITY
- Tests for each batch: diameter, tensile strength, modulus, bond, glass content, Tg, water absorption.
- An auto-generated **test certificate** for each dispatch.
- A record of third-party / NABL lab reports with expiry reminders.
- NCRs and customer complaints → root cause → corrective action, each with an owner and a due date.

### 4.6 FINANCE
- **Invoice sync with Tally**: invoices are raised in Tally (or the system pushes them there) and pulled into Control Centre.
- **Receivables cockpit:** customer-wise ageing (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+), retention money, promised-payment dates, a collection owner for each invoice, and automatic reminders.
- **Payables:** vendor dues and a payment calendar.
- **Product costing:** live cost per metre for each diameter (RM + power + labour + overhead), margin per order and a minimum-price guard on quotes.
- **Cash flow:** a 13-week forecast from expected collections minus committed payments.
- **Expenses:** field travel and site visit claims with approval.
- **Monthly P&L view** by product line (GFRP vs BFRP, manufactured vs traded).

### 4.7 OWNER COCKPIT
One screen, refreshed live:
- Today: orders received, metres produced, dispatches, collections.
- Pipeline value by stage. Tenders closing this week.
- Receivables overdue and the top 10 defaulters.
- Plant: plan vs actual, scrap %, RM stock days.
- People: overdue tasks by person and the weekly scorecard.
- Alerts that need the owner's decision (discount approvals, large POs, escalations).

## 5. Phase-wise Roadmap

| Phase | Name | What gets built | Why this order | Indicative time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Foundation** | Login & roles, masters (products/SKUs by diameter & type, customers, contacts, vendors, users), org chart | Everything else depends on clean master data | 1–2 weeks |
| **1** | **Accountability Core** | Task engine with escalations, daily work report, KRA/KPI setup, meeting MoM → tasks, basic scorecard | This is the owner's biggest pain, and it creates the habit of working in the system | 2–3 weeks |
| **2** | **Sales Engine** | Leads, projects, influencers, follow-ups, quotation builder with PDF, order booking, tender tracker, sales dashboard | Revenue is the lifeline, and orders feed operations | 3–4 weeks |
| **3** | **Operations & Quality** | Production planning, shift production log, inventory (RM/FG), purchase, batch QC & test certificates, dispatch | Turns orders into delivered material with traceability | 4–5 weeks |
| **4** | **Finance & Collections** | Tally sync, receivables & collection follow-up, costing per metre, cash-flow forecast, expenses | Cash control on top of real order and dispatch data | 3–4 weeks |
| **5** | **Marketing Engine** | GFRP calculator (web + internal), lead-source ROI, specifier programme, content library, campaigns, map view | Scales demand once the sales process can absorb leads | 3 weeks |
| **6** | **Owner Cockpit & Automation** | CEO dashboard, WhatsApp/email alerts, automatic weekly reports, AI assistant ("show me overdue collections in Gujarat") | Pulls all modules together into "control from one place" | 2–3 weeks |

Each phase is used live for 2–4 weeks and refined before the next one starts.

## 6. Recommended Technology

| Layer | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| App | Web app (Next.js), installable on phones (PWA) | Works on any phone or laptop with no app-store hassle |
| Database & login | Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth + file storage) | Reliable, low cost, role-based security, stores certificates and photos |
| Hosting | Vercel | Simple deployment, custom domain (e.g. `control.aptechnologies.in`) |
| Accounting | Keep Tally; integrate | No disruption to statutory accounting and GST |
| Alerts | WhatsApp Business API + email | The team already lives on WhatsApp |
| Language | English UI with optional Hindi/regional labels | For shop-floor adoption |

## 7. How We Fix Accountability (the operating rhythm)

| When | What | Who | System support |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily 9:30 | Morning plan (top 3 tasks) | Everyone | DWR form, auto-lists overdue tasks |
| Daily 6:00 pm | Evening report | Everyone | DWR actuals, production/shift data auto-filled |
| Daily 6:30 pm | Exceptions summary | Owner | Auto WhatsApp: overdue items, misses, red KPIs |
| Weekly (Mon) | Department reviews | Heads + owner | Auto-generated agenda & scorecards; MoM → tasks |
| Monthly | Business review | Leadership | P&L, pipeline, collections, people scorecards |
| Quarterly | KRA reset & incentives | Owner + HR | Scorecard history |

Rule: **a task with no owner and no due date is not allowed to exist.**

## 8. Information Needed Before Phase 0

1. **Team:** list of people, roles and who reports to whom (sales, production, QC, accounts, dispatch, purchase).
2. **Products:** diameters, types (ribbed / sand-coated, GFRP / BFRP), standard lengths, bends/stirrups offered, and how you price (per metre / per kg).
3. **Current tools:** Tally version? Excel sheets? Any CRM? Samples of the quotation, invoice and test certificate formats.
4. **Plant:** number of pultrusion lines, shifts, approximate monthly capacity, and key raw materials and vendors.
5. **Sales split:** % government/NHAI EPC vs private vs dealers, and manufactured vs traded.
6. **Biggest pains today:** the top 5 recurring problems (e.g., "follow-ups missed", "don't know stock", "payments stuck").
7. **Languages** your shop-floor and field team are comfortable with.
