# Marketing funnel: contractors in Maharashtra and Karnataka

## The funnel (stages and what moves a prospect forward)
1. **Find** a company that does drains, culverts, crash barriers, flooring or sewage work. Put it in Control Centre: Leads > Growth > Prospects.
2. **First touch** (day 0): one personal WhatsApp or email with one useful document (the MoRTH circular and IRC:137 extract) and one question ("which drain or culvert works do you have coming up?").
3. **Follow-up** (days 3, 7, 12): one message each, adding something new each time (a site photo, the weight table, a short factory video). The app already reminds you on these days for quotes; use the same rhythm for prospects.
4. **Interested**: they reply or ask for a price. Tap "Became a lead". It enters Leads and the Funnel view.
5. **Quote** (Control Centre): send the quote PDF on WhatsApp.
6. **Order**, then **repeat**: ask for the next project when the first is delivered.

Track it in the app: Leads > Funnel shows enquiry, contacted, quoted, ordered. Leads > Growth shows which source brings orders.

## Where real contacts come from (allowed sources)
- Government lists published for the public: contractor enlistment lists of the Karnataka PWD and the Maharashtra PWD, and tender award notices on the Karnataka e-procurement portal, the Maharashtra tenders portal, NHAI and GeM. These give company names and project details.
- A company's own website or its published sales or enquiry contact.
- Contractor and builder association meetings, exhibitions, trade fairs, and your own site visits. Ask for a business card or a WhatsApp number in person.
- Your website form, the QR code, Instagram and WhatsApp enquiries (they asked to hear from you).
- Referrals from the contractors you already supply.

## Rules that protect you
- Send each message yourself, one at a time (tap to send). No bulk WhatsApp tools and no purchased lists. WhatsApp bans numbers that bulk-message, and your own business number is the one that matters.
- Do not copy phone numbers or emails in bulk from IndiaMART, JustDial, TradeIndia or similar sites. Their terms forbid it, and people there did not agree to marketing from you.
- Telephone calls and SMS: check the National Do Not Disturb register before cold calling or texting.
- Business contacts only (company numbers and work emails). Note where you found each contact, and delete or stop messaging anyone who asks you to.
- Every email carries your company name, address and a line: "Reply STOP if you do not want more emails."
