Why GFRP

Built for structures where steel fails first.

Non-corrosive

No rust, even in salt water, sewage or chloride-rich soil. No cover spalling, fewer repairs.

Lightweight

About one quarter the weight of steel. Faster handling on site and lower transport cost.

High tensile strength

Higher tensile strength than steel, at a fraction of the weight. Values are in our test certificates.

Non-conductive

Electrically and magnetically neutral, which suits substations, hospitals, MRI rooms and rail.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is GFRP approved for highway and government projects?

GFRP reinforcement is covered by international FRP design codes and is increasingly specified in Indian infrastructure. Approval is project-specific and is given by the consultant or authority engineer. We provide the test reports and technical data they need.

Can GFRP replace steel one-to-one?

Not automatically. GFRP has a different stiffness from steel, so bar size and spacing are designed by the structural engineer. In many slabs, drains and walls the design works out lighter and more economical than steel over the structure's life.

Can bars be bent on site?

No. Bends, stirrups and custom shapes are manufactured in our factory to your bar bending schedule.

How is it tied and placed?

Much like steel, using plastic or coated tie wire and standard chairs. Because the bars are light, placement is faster.

Do you supply test certificates?

Yes. Every supply comes with test certificates, and third-party lab reports can be arranged on request.

Where do you deliver?

Across India, delivered to your site. Tell us the location and quantity for a delivered price.
